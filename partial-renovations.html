When a partial renovation makes sense

A partial renovation is often the best value option, but only when the conditions are right. Here's when it makes sense.

  • No signs of leaks

    The existing waterproofing is performing, with no drummy tiles, damp or stains nearby.

  • The layout works

    You're happy with where the shower, toilet and vanity sit, and don't need to move plumbing.

  • Floors are sound

    Floor tiles are in good condition and a style you can work with or happily keep.

  • You need it done sooner

    A focused scope means less time with your bathroom out of action.

  • Budget matters

    Spend on the changes you'll notice most, without paying for a full strip-out.

  • Preparing to sell or lease

    A fresh, modern bathroom can make a big difference to how a property presents.

How a partial renovation works

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a partial and a full renovation?

A full renovation strips the bathroom back completely and rebuilds everything. A partial renovation updates selected elements (for example wall tiles, vanity, tapware or screen) while keeping things that are in good condition, such as the floor or layout.

Can you match new tiles to my existing ones?

Exact matches can be hard to find, particularly with older tiles. Often a better result comes from choosing a complementary tile that works with what's staying. We'll talk through the options with you.

Will I still need waterproofing?

If we're removing tiles in a wet area such as a shower or over a bath, yes. We re-waterproof those areas before retiling. It's an essential step that we never skip.

What if you find a problem once work starts?

If we uncover something unexpected, like a failed membrane or water damage, we'll stop, show you, and talk through the options before going any further. Nothing extra happens without your approval.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How much does a full bathroom renovation cost?

The cost depends on the size of the room, any layout or plumbing changes, the condition behind the existing finishes and the fixtures you choose.

We provide a fixed, itemised quote after an on-site visit so you know exactly what's included.

Can you change the layout of my bathroom?

Often, yes. Moving a shower, toilet or vanity can make a big difference to how a room works. We'll look at the existing plumbing and structure on site and talk you through what's practical.

Do you renovate ensuites as well as main bathrooms?

Yes. We renovate ensuites, main bathrooms and powder rooms. Smaller spaces benefit most from careful planning, and we'll help you make the most of every centimetre.

Will I need to choose all the fixtures myself?

Only if you want to. We're happy to recommend tiles, tapware, vanities and screens that suit your space and budget, or work with selections you've already made.

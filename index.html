FAQs

Common questions

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Sydney?

Every bathroom is different. The size of the room, the condition behind the tiles, plumbing changes and the fixtures you choose all affect the price. That's why we quote after seeing the space in person.

After an on-site visit you'll receive a fixed, itemised quote so you know exactly what's included before any work begins.

How long does a full bathroom renovation take?

Timeframes depend on scope, but we give you a realistic schedule up front and keep you updated at every stage. Waterproofing needs proper curing time, and we never rush it. It's the part of a bathroom you can't see but rely on most.

Is waterproofing included?

Yes. Waterproofing is included in every renovation that involves wet areas, and it's done before any tiling starts. We never skip it or cut corners on it.

Do I need to supply my own fixtures and tiles?

You can, but you don't have to. We're happy to help you choose tiles, tapware, vanities and screens that suit your space, style and budget, or work with selections you've already made.

Can I use my bathroom during the renovation?

For a full renovation the room will be out of action while we work. If it's your only bathroom, talk to us during the quote and we'll plan the schedule to keep the disruption as short as possible.

Do you clean up afterwards?

Always. We protect floors and access paths during the job, keep the site tidy as we go and finish with a thorough clean, so your new bathroom is ready to use.

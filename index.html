Bathroom renovation cost in Sydney

Every bathroom is different, so we don't publish a one-size-fits-all price. After a free visit to measure up, you get an itemised quote that shows exactly where the money goes, including allowances for tiles and fixtures.

What changes the cost of a bathroom renovation:

  • The size and layout of the room
  • Your choice of tiles, tapware, vanity and screen
  • How much plumbing and electrical work is needed
  • Moving the shower, toilet or vanity to a new spot
  • The condition of the walls and floor once the old tiles are off
  • Built-in storage and joinery

We'll point out the big cost drivers and where you can save. If something changes once work starts, nothing extra goes ahead without your approval.

Our renovation process, step by step

Every job follows the same steps, so you know what's happening and when, and the disruption to your home is kept short.

1. Free on-site consultation

We come to you, measure up and talk through what you want, what's possible in the space and roughly what it will involve.

2. Design and quote

You get an itemised quote with allowances for tiles and fixtures, plus help choosing finishes that suit your budget.

3. Trades coordination

We book the plumber, electrician, waterproofer and tiler in the right order, so you're not chasing anyone.

4. Build

Strip-out, rough-in, waterproofing, tiling and fit-off, with updates from us as each stage is finished.

5. Final walk-through

We go over the finished bathroom with you, sort out anything that isn't right and leave the place clean.

Choosing a bathroom renovator in Sydney

Before you pay a deposit, ask these questions:

  • Are you licensed and insured? Ask for the builder's licence number and check it on the NSW Fair Trading website.
  • Is the quote itemised? A detailed quote shows what's included and makes it easy to compare.
  • Who does the waterproofing? Ask how the wet areas will be waterproofed and whether you'll get a certificate.
  • What do past clients say? Read reviews about communication, tidiness and finishing on time.

We're happy to go through all of this with you before you commit to anything.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Sydney?

Every bathroom is different. The size of the room, the condition behind the tiles, plumbing changes and the fixtures you choose all affect the price. That's why we quote after seeing the space in person.

After an on-site visit you'll receive a fixed, itemised quote so you know exactly what's included before any work begins.

How long does a full bathroom renovation take?

Timeframes depend on scope, but we give you a realistic schedule up front and keep you updated at every stage. Waterproofing needs proper curing time, and we never rush it. It's the part of a bathroom you can't see but rely on most.

Is waterproofing included?

Yes. Waterproofing is included in every renovation that involves wet areas, and it's done before any tiling starts. We never skip it or cut corners on it.

Do I need to supply my own fixtures and tiles?

You can, but you don't have to. We're happy to help you choose tiles, tapware, vanities and screens that suit your space, style and budget, or work with selections you've already made.

Can I use my bathroom during the renovation?

For a full renovation the room will be out of action while we work. If it's your only bathroom, talk to us during the quote and we'll plan the schedule to keep the disruption as short as possible.

Do you clean up afterwards?

Always. We protect floors and access paths during the job, keep the site tidy as we go and finish with a thorough clean, so your new bathroom is ready to use.

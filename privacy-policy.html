Overview

OZTECH Bathrooms (“OZTECH”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and disclose your personal information when you visit our website, contact us, request a quote or engage us to carry out work.

We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), to the extent they apply to our business. By using our website or providing us with your personal information, you agree to it being handled as described in this policy.

Information we collect

The personal information we collect depends on how you interact with us. It may include:

  • your name, phone number, email address and postal or site address;
  • details about your property and project, including photos, measurements and preferences you share with us;
  • information you provide in enquiries, quote requests, emails, phone calls or messages;
  • billing and payment details needed to invoice and receive payment for our services;
  • feedback and reviews you choose to provide; and
  • technical information about your visit to our website, such as your browser type, device, pages viewed and approximate location, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive information (such as health information). Please do not send us sensitive information unless it is necessary for us to provide our services.

How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you wherever reasonably practicable, for example, when you fill in our quote form, phone or email us, meet us on site or engage us to complete work. We may also collect information from third parties such as builders, real estate agents, strata managers or other people you have authorised to deal with us on your behalf.

When you submit a quote request or enquiry through our website, the details you enter are stored securely by our website hosting and database provider and sent to us by email through our email delivery provider, so we can respond to you.

How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to your enquiries and provide quotes;
  • arrange site visits, plan, schedule and carry out renovation work;
  • communicate with you about your project, including progress updates;
  • issue invoices, process payments and keep business records;
  • manage warranties, defects and after-care;
  • improve our website, services and customer experience;
  • comply with our legal and regulatory obligations; and
  • with your consent, send you occasional information about our services. You can opt out at any time.

Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose it to:

  • tradespeople, contractors and suppliers who help us deliver your project (for example plumbers, electricians, glaziers and product suppliers);
  • certifiers, insurers and government authorities where required for your project or by law;
  • service providers who support our business, such as accounting, IT, website hosting and email providers; and
  • professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants.

We only share what is reasonably necessary, and we expect those parties to handle your information appropriately. Some of our service providers (for example cloud email or hosting services) may store data outside Australia. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information is protected.

Project photos

We may photograph our work before, during and after a project for quality records and to show examples of our work on our website and social media. We take care not to include personal items, people or identifying details such as street numbers in photos we publish. If you would prefer that photos of your project are not published, just let us know.

Cookies & analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to make the site work, remember your preferences and understand how visitors use it. We may use third-party services, such as web analytics or font providers, which may collect technical information like your IP address and browser details.

You can set your browser to refuse or delete cookies. Some parts of the website may not work as intended if you do.

Storage & security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification and disclosure. When information is no longer needed for any purpose for which it may be used or disclosed, and we are not required by law to keep it, we take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it.

No method of transmitting information over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee the security of information you send to us online.

Access & correction

You can ask to access the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it if you believe it is inaccurate, out of date or incomplete. Please contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity first. In limited circumstances permitted by law we may decline a request, and if so we will explain why.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page, with the date it was last updated shown at the top.
